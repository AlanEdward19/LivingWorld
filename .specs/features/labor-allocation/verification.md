## Deviations judged

- AC 5 (C15): honest. The wheat shock cannot work in loader worlds (food from plants), a 20-seed control/treatment now exists, the claim "at least 18 of 20" is exactly what runs. It still differs from AC 5 (water instead of wheat, share days 100-299 instead of "headcount on day 180"). Confound: farm workers eat the farm stock through the new ration (C14), so with a dry well non-farm workers can die first and the farm share rises by survivorship without any job move; the test asserts no `LaborAllocation` move. Plausible, not shown. Not a false claim.
- AC 8 "at 90 days" to "within 90 days": justified (oscillation near 60 days), recorded, weakens the check; unchanged since Round 1.
- AC 9: recorded with causes, but the criterion is not met as written. 348 / 373 = 93.3% < 95%; C10's floor allows 313 (84% of 373). The causal story (1) wage accident, (2) thirst at the farm fixed by the ration, (3) unpriced stock fixed by C13 is supported for (2) by C14 and for (3) by C13; (1) and the reverted daily-wage experiment (276) are asserted in prose and not reproducible from the tree. The numbers 325 -> 343 -> 348 and 373 are consistent with what I measured (325 at 7c4ea25, 348 at HEAD). Verdict: weakened, recorded, true.
- AC 10: metric replaced (20 NPC / 3 seeds / 10 years to 40 NPC / 8 seeds / 5 years) and recorded. The replacement is meaningful: pre-labor 112, HEAD 111, so AC 10 (>=100% of before) is met within 1% on the new metric. The claim "live world still loses most people (40 to about 14)" is true (111/8 = 13.9) and equally true before the feature (112), so "not a target of this feature" holds. The claim "open finding, filed as an issue" is not supported: `gh issue list` shows only #20 trade-ledger, #21 snapshot binary, #22 this closure; no issue for the live-world population loss. Unverifiable claim in Deviations.
- AC 12: numbers are true (657 KB to 542 KB, 0.725 to 0.68 within noise, +7.5% over 504 KB, -17% vs develop). But "AC 12 closed" overstates: C12 is now relative to 7c4ea25, which already contains the labor feature and the new building occupancy system, so it measures the perf commit and the ration, not the feature's own allocation cost; against the original reference (504 KB) the feature is +7.5%, which exceeds the AC's 102% and sits inside the previously renegotiated 110%.
